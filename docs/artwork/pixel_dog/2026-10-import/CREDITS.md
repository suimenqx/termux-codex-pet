Imported from [Dog by rmazanek](https://opengameart.org/content/dog-3)
(2022-06-27), marked CC0 by its contributor.

- Source file: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/dog_medium.png
- SHA-256: `77a32e17840c921f939a754cc5d73622334e568a0bc2c40d55d534b487d2a58a`
- Parent: [Husky Sprites by Hellkipz](https://opengameart.org/content/husky-sprites), CC0.
- Original: [German Shepherd by Shepardskin](https://opengameart.org/content/german-shepherd-0), CC0.
- Original animation archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/German%20Shepherd.zip

Modifications: fixed 60×38 cells copied to transparent 64×64 canvases at (2,13),
without scaling, repainting, interpolation or per-frame alignment. The unused
walk row and blank cells are omitted. Product states map to the supplied stand,
run, bark and sit poses. The original German Shepherd GIFs supply the run
(130 ms) and bark (150 ms) exposures; other timings are integration choices.

Reproduction and inspection: `tools/import_pixel_dog.py` and
`docs/artwork/pixel_dog/2026-10-import/` in the termux-codex-pet repository.
`import-record.json` records source rectangles and exported PNG/RGBA hashes.
